On Tuesday as I was wandering about Ottawa, I stumbled across Camera Trading Company on Bank street. They had a whole bunch of old and new camera equipment. It was a really cool store. There were three gentlemen and I chatted briefly with each of them. This gentleman, and unfortunately I didn’t get his name, offered his lens for me to try. It was a 25mm or 28mm lens with an M-mount adapter. I tried it on and took this shot. If you find yourself in Ottawa and interested in photography, I recommend checking them out.
