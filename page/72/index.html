Published on Jan 22, 2020

As of the last several years, I’ve spent a lot less time gaming than before. I’m basically an occasional gamer. It is with that in mind that I recently sold my PS4 which was lying unused, and bought a subscription to Apple Arcade that let’s me play games on several devices and for short windows of time.

I’ve played a few games on it now, and by far my favorite has been “WHAT THE GOLF?”. I don’t think I’ve had as much fun playing a game in a while. I fire it up a few times a week on my iPhone and make a little progress each time. It is interesting, inventive, clever, and above all, hilarious. I’m 77% through the game and savoring the last of it. I hope this team makes more games and I look forward to them.
