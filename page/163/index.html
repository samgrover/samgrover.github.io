FIFA officials, please ban the Vuvuzela

FIFA officials, please ban the Vuvuzela

My fondest memories of the World Cup have the soundtrack of a stadium full of fans chanting for their team and for the sport. All I can hear in the current World Cup is the constant annoying sound of thousands of Vuvuzelas. When I sat down to watch the the first match I wasn’t expecting that and thought there was something wrong with the video streaming. Unfortunately that’s not the case and instead of hearing the emotion in the stadium all we hear is an annoying buzz. I bet the broadcasters are losing viewership over this.
