Tripod, a new podcast player

Tripod launched about a week ago but I only started using it over the weekend. After two days and several podcast episodes I am 100% on board with it.

I am blown away by the set of features that it has launched with on day one. It has everything I’ve come to expect from a podcast app, and more. As a developer, I’m also blown away by Ben’s statement that it took only 31 days to develop it. All of this gives me a lot of hope for excellent indie apps made with AI assistance. I would love to hear more about the process.

It’s the podcast app I didn’t know I needed, even after a long time with my previous one, Overcast. There is still plenty of trial left, but I’ve already added it to my budget tracker sheet.

The icon and name have felt a little odd, but I’m no expert on these things, so 🤷

I’ve never used the big apps, like Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or YouTube for podcasts. If you use those, I don’t know if a small paid indie app would make sense to you, but if you’re curious, give it a shot!

I’ve used several of Ben’s apps and they’ve all been great. Croissant is useful nearly every day. I use Obscura on and off, and it makes great photos, but it has never stuck with me, but then no third-party photography app has ever stuck with me (currently using No Fusion), but I digress…
